# Daerdree Bar & Timeclub — Larnaca, Cyprus

## Overview
Daerdree is an atmospheric board game bar and timeclub located in Larnaca, Cyprus. The venue combines over 50 board games, craft cocktails, a cozy lounge, and a full-service bar — making it a unique social destination on the island.

## What We Offer
- **Board Games**: 50+ board games — Monopoly, Uno, Chess, Settlers of Catan, Carcassonne, and many more. Free for bar guests.
- **Cocktails & Bar**: Craft and classic cocktails, wine, beer, and non-alcoholic beverages.
- **Menu**: Snacks, appetizers, and main dishes — ideal for sharing with friends.
- **Events**: Weekly public events (trivia nights, game tournaments, open mic, DJ sets) and private events (birthday parties, corporate gatherings, bachelor/bachelorette parties) with full catering options.
- **Catering**: Off-site catering service for private parties and corporate events across Cyprus.
- **Private Hire**: Private room with a separate entrance available for groups — includes a personal bartender, curated games, and custom menu.

## For Beginners
No experience needed. Staff will recommend games based on your group size and experience level, and help with the rules.
